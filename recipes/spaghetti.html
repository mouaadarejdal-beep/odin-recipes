Spaghetti

Description

A hearty Italian classic featuring al dente spaghetti tossed in a rich, slow-simmered beef and tomato sauce.

Ingrediants

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 400g ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, black pepper, and dried oregano to taste
  • Freshly grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat, add the diced onion and minced garlic, and sauté until translucent.
  2. Add ground beef, breaking it up with a spoon, and cook until fully browned.
  3. Stir in tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, oregano, salt, and black pepper. Lower heat and simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, add spaghetti, and cook until al dente.
  5. Drain pasta, toss directly with the sauce, and serve hot topped with grated Parmesan.
